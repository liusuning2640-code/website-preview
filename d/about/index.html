03Master’s thesis: AI in education

Generative AI in Chinese Higher Vocational Education: A Systematic Review of Educational Purposes in English-Language Research

  1. 894records identified
  2. 743screened
  3. 20studies included

A systematic review of English-language research through Biesta's three functions of education. Drawing on 894 records identified, 743 screened, 20 studies included, it finds that GenAI is predominantly framed as a tool for performance improvement (qualification), with limited attention to socialisation and no direct engagement with subjectification — student agency, judgment, and responsibility remain underexamined.
